About us

A forward-thinking partner for people, enterprise and environment

Hatzalah Africa Company Limited is dedicated to advancing human capital growth, economic development and environmental resilience across Africa and beyond. We believe people are the cornerstone of progress; by equipping individuals, businesses and communities with the right skills and opportunities, we create inclusive and sustainable economic growth.

We work with governments, corporates, NGOs and communities to deliver innovative solutions in workforce development, enterprise support, economic transformation, clean energy promotion and carbon credit monetization.

We are the vital link connecting talent to opportunity, and green solutions to sustainable financing, because nurturing human potential and protecting our environment is all that matters for Africa to achieve its Agenda 2063 goals.

Climate Action & Clean Energy Hatzalah

Clean Cooking Promotion

Deploying the Village Savings and Loan Association (VSLA) Clean Energy Financing Model to overcome the upfront cost barrier for LPG and improved cookstoves, and connecting communities with reliable suppliers (Lake Gas, Taifa Gas, etc.).

Carbon Credit Advisory & Monetization

Working with international carbon registries to aggregate Verified Emission Reductions (VERs) from clean cooking projects, ensuring communities receive direct financial benefits from carbon offset revenues.

Green Livelihoods

Linking clean energy adoption with sustainable livelihoods, including tree nurseries, beekeeping, briquette production and youth-led clean energy maintenance enterprises.

Flagship program

Women at Hatzalah Initiative

A flagship program designed to advance women's leadership across economic, social and environmental sectors. Because women are the primary household energy managers, this initiative anchors our clean cooking program through a holistic approach:

1

Leadership Academies

Training in governance, negotiation and green entrepreneurship.

2

Mentorship Networks

Pairing emerging women leaders with established figures in energy and policy.

3

Mental Health & Well-being Support

Workshops on stress management, resilience and mindfulness.

4

Pipeline to Leadership

Reserved placements for program graduates in clean energy enterprises and VSLA management, ensuring women lead the transition to clean cooking and control the carbon credit revenues generated by their households.

A message from our CEO

Ms. Rosemary Chambe Jairo Founder & Chief Executive Officer
"Unlocking Human Potential, Protecting Our Planet"

At Hatzalah Africa Company Limited, we believe that Africa's greatest resource is its people. The strength of our continent lies not only in its natural wealth but in the untapped potential of its human capital. Our mission is to unlock that potential by equipping individuals, businesses and communities with the skills, opportunities and resources they need to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

In an era defined by innovation, climate change and global competitiveness, human capacity development is no longer optional – it is essential. We have expanded our mandate to include Clean Energy Promotion, recognizing that economic growth cannot occur at the expense of environmental health. By promoting clean cooking solutions, we are tackling deforestation, improving public health and mitigating indoor air pollution.

Furthermore, we are pioneering Carbon Credit Mechanisms that convert these environmental actions into sustainable revenue streams for local communities. This ensures that communities are not just adopting clean energy, but are financially rewarded for their climate-positive actions. By investing in education, green skills, entrepreneurship and inclusive economic systems, we are laying the foundation for sustainable prosperity that benefits generations to come.

As CEO, I am confident that with determination, collaboration and strategic investment in people and climate resilience, Hatzalah Africa Company Limited will continue to play a pivotal role in driving human capital development, economic transformation and environmental sustainability across the continent.

Rosemary Chambe Jairo
